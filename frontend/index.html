WebCardio WC1340 Wi-Fi Connection Guide

1

Host a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi Hotspot on Mac or Phone

The WebCardio WC1340 uses an 802.11b radio with pre-programmed credentials:

Hotspot SSID: CMSEN (or try WC1340 or full barcode WC134000000T0010PHNTCMSEN...)
Hotspot Password: copernicus (LifeSignals standard WPA2-PSK key)
Band: 2.4 GHz Only (Enable "Maximize Compatibility" on iPhone/Android)
2

Power On Biosensor

Press and hold the power button on the center of the patch until the LED illuminates or blinks. The patch will connect to the hotspot within 5–15 seconds.

3

Scan & Discover Sensor on Network

Click "Scan Local Subnet" to search for the sensor's IP address.
4

Direct IP Connection (Optional)

If you know your sensor's IP address or if it is running in SoftAP mode:

Backend listening on all interfaces on TCP 5000 and UDP 5000 / 9000.
